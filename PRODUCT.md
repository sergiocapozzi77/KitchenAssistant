# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A household cook using a kitchen-mounted or countertop display while planning
or preparing meals.

## Product Purpose

KitchenAssistant helps a household manage kitchen inventory and browse
cookbooks and recipes. Success means cooks can read ingredients and cooking
instructions quickly and comfortably while preparing food.

## Brand Personality

Warm, practical, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered, hard-to-read screens and interfaces that require precise
tapping while cooking.

## Design Principles

- Make recipe content easy to scan while cooking.
- Favor readable text at arm's length.
- Keep interactions practical for a kitchen environment.
- Present information clearly without relying on color alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize readable text at arm's length, strong contrast, and large touch
targets. Do not rely on color alone to communicate information.
